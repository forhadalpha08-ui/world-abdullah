BIOSPHERE FLUXES

Net Primary Productivity & Carbon Reservoirs

Global Net Primary Production (NPP) fixes approximately 105 Petagrams of Carbon per year. Terrestrial forests and soils sequester 3.1 ± 0.6 GtC/yr, while the marine biological pump exports 2.9 ± 0.4 GtC/yr into the bathypelagic ocean. Spaceborne instruments continuously monitor global photosynthetic health.

MODIS / SENTINEL-2 Optical Multispectral NDVI

Monitors global vegetation vigor, leaf area index (LAI), and drought stress at 10–250m spatial resolution.

OCO-2 / OCO-3 Solar-Induced Fluorescence

Measures real-time photosynthetic photon emission (SIF) to quantify crop yields and global carbon assimilation.

TROPOMI (SENTINEL-5P) Atmospheric Trace Gases

Tracks global methane (CH₄) plumes, carbon monoxide (CO), and tropospheric nitrogen dioxide (NO₂) emissions daily.

NASA GEDI LIDAR Canopy Structure & Biomass

Spaceborne laser altimeter on the ISS mapping three-dimensional forest canopy height and aboveground carbon storage.
